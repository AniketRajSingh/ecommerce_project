COLD PRESSED OIL

Manufactured by Chemical Process:

Manufactured by chemical process: Very harsh chemicals like Hexane, N-Heptane, Neutralizers, Bleach, Anti-foaming agents are being used during extraction.

No Chemicals / Preservatives added during process:

No Chemicals / Preservatives added during process: Manufactured by natural process by applying only mechanical pressure.

Higher up to 350°C:

Higher up to 350°C: Subjected to higher temperatures during extraction and refining, which leads to the loss of heat-sensitive nutrients.

Lower than 60°C:

Processed at lower temperatures, preserving the oil's natural properties and preventing the degradation of heat-sensitive compounds like antioxidants and vitamins.

Nutritional value lost:

Nutritional value lost: Lighter color and no aroma, refining process results in the loss of nutritional components present in the raw material.

High in Nutritional Value:

High in Nutritional Value: Retains more natural antioxidants, vitamins, and minerals due to the gentle extraction process. It often has a richer flavor and a deeper color.

Chemical Additives present:

Chemical Additives present: Contain additives, preservatives, and antioxidants added during the refining process to improve shelf life and stability.

No chemical Additives:

No chemical Additives: Typically free from chemical additives and preservatives since it undergoes minimal processing.

Bad and Rejected seeds:

Bad and Rejected seeds: As refining process is employed, bad quality seeds are used for extraction and refined later through chemical process.

By Process Good seeds:

By Process Good seeds: As no chemicals involved using good quality seeds is mandate and gives us maximum health benefits.

Cheap in cost and Quality:

Cheap in cost and Quality: As refining process allows for mass production and usage of rejected / lower quality seeds.

Value for money with best quality:

Value for money with best quality: Tends to be more expensive due to the labor-intensive, time-consuming extraction process and best quality product.
