Sourcing to Selling Wood Pressed Oils

Sourcing

Our seeds are sourced directly from farmers through various markets like Disa, Amreli, Tiptur, Rajkot, Latur, Vijapur etc.

Seed Cleaning

Seeds go through destoner and gravity separator to remove any dust particles, stones, heavy met.al, straw or other impurities

Extraction

Oil is extracted in GMP certified hygienic environment with utmost condition to avoid contamination. After extraction, oil is settled for 2 days and filtered through 10 micron cotton filter.

Testing

Every bottle going out is tested by expert food technologists in our inhouse state of the art laboratory.

Dispatch

To minimize handling and storages in multiple places, we pack and ship the boxes directly from our own dispatch center. This ensures the minimum handling of the bottles.
